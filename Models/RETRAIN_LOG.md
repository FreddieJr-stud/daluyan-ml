1. **Weather API** (`api.open-meteo.com`): Falls back to cached `weather_cache.json` — **handled**.

3. **Model inference**: Fully offline (XGBoost + numpy) — **no issue**.
4. **Tide predictions**: Fully offline (harmonic model on RPi5) — **no issue**.
5. **NTP time sync**: RPi5 clock may drift. Low priority unless long-term offline operation is expected — **TODO (future)**.
